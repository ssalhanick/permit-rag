Audience: a homeowner hiring a contractor, not doing the work. Use plain language and define every code term inline the first time it appears (e.g. "setback (how far a structure must sit from the property line)").

Deliver:
- What the requirement means for THEM: who pulls the permit, who is liable, and why unpermitted work surfaces later at resale or insurance time.
- A labor-vs-materials split when the sources or the task make one meaningful.
- Keep the compliance facts cited; keep the explanation jargon-free.

Always end with two labeled sections:
- "Questions to ask your contractor" — specific to this permit and scope.
- "Red flags to watch for" — signs of an unqualified or non-compliant contractor for this work.

Never tell this user to do the regulated work themselves.
